Part VI is a practical guide to pstack based on [Part 1](https://x.com/poteto/status/2094457600259842065) and [Part 2](https://x.com/poteto/status/2097732320606507506) of *The Complete Guide to pstack*, the guide that poteto, the author of pstack, posted on X.

The guide covers "<strong>when you use pstack in your own project, where to start and in what order to proceed</strong>."

Part VI explains the content of the guide in the following order.

1. <strong>Build a verification skill</strong> (Part 1, [Chapter 36](https://zenn.dev/sc30gsw/books/7ff701b9811d04/viewer/c9901e))
2. <strong>Make the agent understand what the problem is, and have it take over past work</strong> (Part 2, [Chapter 37](https://zenn.dev/sc30gsw/books/7ff701b9811d04/viewer/6a59d6))
3. <strong>Design from how users use the product, and try it with prototypes</strong> (Part 2, [Chapter 38](https://zenn.dev/sc30gsw/books/7ff701b9811d04/viewer/006bc8))
4. <strong>Settle the design and turn it into a plan you can carry out</strong> (Part 2, [Chapter 39](https://zenn.dev/sc30gsw/books/7ff701b9811d04/viewer/f87769))

Part 1 names the <strong>verification skill</strong> as the first thing the user builds, and covers how to build and use it.

Part 2 covers how the user and the agent decide together what to build, once the verification skill exists. This book organizes the content of Part 2 into 13 key points and explains them across three chapters, Chapter 37 through Chapter 39.

<aside class="msg message"><span class="msg-symbol">!</span><div class="msg-content">
<p class="code-line" data-line="16"><strong>Verification skill</strong>: a project-specific Skill the agent uses to start and operate the user's app and verify that a change works. For example, if you changed the settings screen, the agent clicks the gear button, opens the settings screen, and saves a screenshot of the changed part as evidence.</p>
</div></aside>

The verification skill comes first for one reason. <strong>As long as the agent cannot verify its own changes, a person has to check both whether a prototype is any good and whether a plan is done</strong>.

In that case, the agent's work cannot move forward until a person finishes checking. The person becomes the bottleneck that stops the work, and spends the day watching the agent.

<a id="what-you-learn-in-part-vi"></a>


## What you learn in Part VI

After reading Part VI, you will know the following.

- How to build a verification skill for your own app, and how to keep it up to date as the app changes
- How to make the agent understand what the problem is the way a person does, and how to have it take over past context
- How to start design from how users use the product, and how to pick one of several prototype options
- How to revise the design based on what you learned during implementation, and how to turn it into a plan split into small PRs you can verify
